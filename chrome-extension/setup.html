Connect Beam to Chrome

Beam's Browser Tabs needs its small extension in Chrome. This takes a minute, once per Chrome profile.

  1. Open chrome://extensions
    Paste it into the address bar and press Enter. (Or: menu ⋮ › Extensions › Manage Extensions.)
  2. Turn on Developer mode, the switch at the top right.
  3. Click Load unpacked, press Ctrl+L, paste this folder and press Enter, then click Select:
  4. That's it. In Beam, Search Browser Tabs now lists your tabs.

Keep Developer mode on: Chrome turns off extensions loaded this way when it is off. To see incognito tabs too, open the extension's Details and turn on "Allow in Incognito".
